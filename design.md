# Design Specifications: Grogg's Traditional Irish Pub

## 1. Overview and Art Direction
**Theme:** Traditional Irish Pub with a modern, clean web layout. 
**Vibe:** Warm, inviting, authentic, and rustic.
**Key Textures:** Deep, rich dark woodgrain backgrounds used throughout the main body of the site to emulate a classic pub interior.

## 2. Color Palette
* **Primary Accent (Irish Green):** `~#157A36` (Used for header background, buttons, price tags, and highlight borders).
* **Primary Background (Dark Wood):** `~#2B1A10` to `~#1A0F0A` (Used as a dark, textured woodgrain backdrop for the main content).
* **Text Primary:** `#FFFFFF` (White) used for high contrast on dark backgrounds.
* **Text Secondary:** `~#D1D1D1` (Light gray/off-white) used for paragraph text and subtle descriptions.

## 3. Typography
* **Headings (H1, H2, H3):** A classic, elegant Serif font (e.g., *Playfair Display*, *Merriweather*, or *Cinzel*). This gives the traditional, established feel. Used for "GROGG'S TRADITIONAL IRISH PUB", "OUR BREWS & SPIRITS", "DAILY SPECIALS", etc.
* **Body Copy (p, a, span):** A clean, modern Sans-Serif font (e.g., *Open Sans*, *Lato*, or *Montserrat*). Provides easy readability for descriptions, menus, and footer info.
* **Text Styling:** Headings are primarily styled in ALL CAPS to command attention, often accompanied by subtle decorative divider lines above and below section titles.

## 4. UI Components & Elements
* **Buttons:** Rectangular with slightly rounded corners (approx. 2px-4px border radius). Solid green background with white sans-serif text.
* **Cards (Menu/Drinks):** * Images are top-aligned, rectangular.
    * Titles are left-aligned Serif, ALL CAPS.
    * Descriptions are left-aligned Sans-serif, standard case.
    * *Hover/Active State:* Demonstrated in the "Brews & Spirits" section, cards feature a subtle green outer glow or border to indicate interaction.
* **Price Tags:** Small, distinct green rectangular blocks with white text displaying the price (e.g., `$11.50`).
* **Dividers:** Minimalist horizontal lines (often flanking a section title) in a muted brown or subtle gold/green hue to separate content blocks without clutter.

## 5. Layout Structure (Top to Bottom)

### Header / Navigation
* **Background:** Solid green.
* **Logo:** Left-aligned. Features a shamrock icon next to "GROGG'S TRADITIONAL IRISH PUB" in a serif font.
* **Links:** Center-right aligned (MENU, EVENTS, GALLERY, CONTACT).
* **CTA:** Far right, highlighted button ("BOOK A TABLE").

### Hero Section
* **Visual:** Full-width, high-quality background image of patrons enjoying drinks (cheerful, social atmosphere).
* **Overlay:** Dark gradient or scrim to ensure text legibility.
* **Copy:** Large H1 "GROGG'S TRADITIONAL IRISH PUB", subheadline "Where Tradition Meets Modern Hospitality".
* **CTA:** "VIEW MENU" button centered.

### Announcement Bar / Marquee
* **Background:** Solid green (matches header).
* **Content:** Scrolling or static inline text highlighting weekly events (e.g., "WHAT'S ON: TRIVIA NIGHT - TUESDAYS | LIVE DJs - FRIDAYS & SATURDAYS | CRAFT BEER TASTING - THURSDAYS").

### Our Brews & Spirits Section
* **Background:** Dark woodgrain.
* **Layout:** 3-column grid.
* **Content:** Image-heavy cards showcasing specific drinks (Guinness, Whiskey Flights, Craft Beers) with brief descriptions.

### Daily Specials Section
* **Background:** Dark woodgrain.
* **Layout:** 3-column grid.
* **Content:** Food imagery (Shepherd's Pie, Fish and Chips, Burgers) with titles, descriptions, and prominent green price blocks.

### Instagram Feed
* **Header:** "FOLLOW US ON INSTAGRAM @groggspub"
* **Layout:** 4-column grid of square social media images seamlessly integrated into the woodgrain background. Features small Instagram icon overlays on the top right of each image.

### Footer
* **Background:** Dark woodgrain.
* **Layout:** 3-column grid (Contact, Hours, Info).
* **Typography:** Contact info and hours are listed in a clean sans-serif font.
* **Bottom Bar:** Solid green bottom border/bar with copyright info and a central shamrock icon.

## 6. Image Style Guidelines
* **Photography:** Warm, moody lighting. High contrast. Food and drink shots should be close-up, appetizing, and professionally styled against dark, rustic backgrounds to match the website's woodgrain.
* **Lifestyle:** Images of people should convey a lively, friendly, and authentic pub atmosphere.
